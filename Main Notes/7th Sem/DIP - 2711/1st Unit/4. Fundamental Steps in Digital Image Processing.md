## 4. Fundamental Steps in Digital Image Processing
- Digital Image Processing include steps that are used to enhance the useful imformation inside of an image.

**1. Image Acquistion**
- Involves the step of capturing an image and storing it as a digital file using sensors and specialized hardware for imagery.
- Quality of this step is directly related to the qualities of other steps.
- Includes preprocessing like scaling.


**2. Image Enhancement**
- This is used to enchance the image usability according to the need , for eg , human interpretation. It invlolves 
	- Adjusting Brightness , saturation
	- Reducing noise.
	- Sharpening Details.

**3. Image Restoration**
- This is used to undo the effects of degrading qualities.
	- Blurring 
	- Noise
	- Distortion
