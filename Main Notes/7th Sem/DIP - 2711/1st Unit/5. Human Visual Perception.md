## 5. Human Visual Perception

**1. Human Eye Structure**
- It has 3 membranes
	- Cornea 
		- The outermost transparent protective layer
	- Choroid
		- Beneath cornea , carries blood vessels that help in nutrition. It's pigmented to reduce stray light.
	- Retina
		- Innermost layer which is sensitive to light.
