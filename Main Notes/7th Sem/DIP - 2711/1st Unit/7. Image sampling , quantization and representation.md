## 7. Image sampling , quantization and representation

**1. Digitization: Image Sampling and quantization**
- To convert analog signals into digital signals , sampling and quantization are applied. 
	- <u>Sampling</u>
		- This process digitizes the spatial coordinates (x,y).
		- It is done by taking discrete , equally spaced measurements creating a grid of samples
			- The quality of the image depends on the number of samples taken defining spatial resolution
		- If low number of samples are used , image artifact called checkerboard is noticed.
		
	- <u>Quantization</u>
		- This process digitizes the intensity of the image. 
		- It is done by converting continous intensity interval into finite set of intensity level.
			- The quality of quantization depends upon the number of intensity levels used , defining the intensity resolution 
		- False contouring happens when low number of gray levels are chosen , this effect produces ridge like structures in smooth areas of the image where no edge existed originally. It is very noticeable in gray levels less than or equal to 16.
