## Origins Of DIP
- A digital image may be defined as a two dimensional function f(x,y) where x and y are the coordinates and amplitude f is the gray level or intensity. 
- Boundaries are not well defined.

**Early Image Transmission**
- Bartlane cable system
	- Earliest coding image transmission was done for transmission of newspapers from london to new york.
	- Goal
		- Reduce tranmission time from week to 3 hours 
	- Mechanism
		- Specialized mechanism coded images and reciever end used telegraph printers to print the image
	- Tonal Quality
		- Earlies it was 5 distinct levels of gray , but in 1929 it was upgraded to 15 
	- This is not considered DIP as no digital processing unit was used.
	- Drawbacks
		- Selection of printing procedures
		- Distribution of intensity levels.



**Birth Of DIP**
- Birth of DIP corresponds with the birth of digital computers
	- In 1940s 
		- John Van Neumann introduced key concepts such as storage of program instructions and data and Conditional Branching which formed the basis of CPUs today. 
	- In 1948 transistor was invented by bell labs
	- In 1958 IC was invented
	- Early 1970s first CPU was invented
	- Early 1980s till now - Lithography techniques such as LSI , VLSI , ULSI was invented
- Up until 1960s , computers became fast enough to carry out image processing tasks. Image processing requires high computational powers and storage.
- First DIP program was developed by NASA for ranger 7 mission in 1964 which was used to correct distortion in the images sent by the probe.


**Major Uses of DIP Today**
- Space Programs
- Medicinal
	- CAT (Computirised Axial Tomography)
		- Invented by Sir Godfrey and Sir Allan for which they shared a nobel prize for.
		- Uses algorithms to construct "slices" using rotating source.
