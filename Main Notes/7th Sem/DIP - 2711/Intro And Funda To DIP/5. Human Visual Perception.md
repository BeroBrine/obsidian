## 5. Human Visual Perception

**1. Human Eye Structure**
- It has 3 membranes
	- Cornea 
		- The outermost transparent protective layer
	- Choroid
		- Beneath cornea , carries blood vessels that help in nutrition. It's pigmented to reduce stray light.
	- Retina
		- Innermost layer which is sensitive to light.

**2. Photoreceptors: Cones Vs Rods**
- Cones
	- Number are in 6-7 million
	- These are concentrated in fovea
	- Responsible for color mapping and sharpness of the image
	- Bright Light Vision
	- Each cone has it's own nerve ending.
- Rods
	- Number are in 75-150 million
	- Distributed over entire retina
	- Responsible for low light vision
	- Several rods are connected to one nerve.


**3. Brightness adaption and discrimination**
- Brightness adaption
	- Eye can adapt to enormous range of light upto order of 10<sup>10</sup>. But it cannot operate on it simulataneously , it adapts to the current global illumination.
- Subjective Brightness.
	- Perceived brightness is a logarithmic function of the intensity of light. This means that human eye is much more susceptible to changes in intensity low light rather than bright light. 
- **Weber Ratio**
	- Weber ratio measures the brightness discrimination
	 $$ K = \frac{\Delta I_c}{I} $$
	 - $$ \Delta I_c $$
		 - This is just the noticable increase in illumination
	- I is the background illumination
	- A small weber ratio means good discrimination (A small change is noticeable) 
	- A large weber ratio means poor discrimination (Large change is needed to be seen).
