## 8. Basic Realtionship between Pixels

**1. Neighbors of a pixel**
- A pixel at (x,y) has 4 horizontal and 4 vertial neighbors.
$$(x+1 , y) , (x-1 , y) , (x , y+1) , (x , y -1)$$
- This set of pixels are called 4-neighbors of p , and denoted by $$N_4(p)$$ 
- Each pixel is unit distance from p.
- Some of the neighbour locations of p lie outside the digital image if p is a border pixel.

- <u>Diagonally aligned neighbors</u>
	- These pixels are diagonally unit distance from the pixel p at (x,y). $$(x+1 , y+1) , (x-1 , y-1) , (x-1 , y+1) , (x+1 , y-1).$$ 
	- this set of pixels are denoted as $$N_D(p)$$
- These diagonally and 4-neighbors combined are called 8-neighbors of p , denoted by $$N_8(p)$$

**Adjacency**
- Let V be the set of intensity values used to define adjacency.
- In a binary image , V = {1} if we are referring to adjacency of pixels with value 1.
- In a grayscale image , V typically contains more elements.
- Three types of adjacency
	- <u>4-adjacency</u>
		- The pixels p and q with values from V are said to be 4 adjacent if q is contained in the set of $$N_4(p)$$ 
	- 8-adjacency 
		- The pixels p and q with values from V are said to be 8 adjacent if q is contained in the set of $$N_8(p)$$ 
	- <u>M-Adjacency</u>
		- Two pixels p and q with values V are said to m adjacent if 
			- q is in N_4(p).
					OR
			- q is in N_D(p) and the set $$N_4(p) \cap N_4(q)$$ has no pixels whose values are from the set V.
			
		- It is used to avoid the ambiguity when 8-adjacency is used.
	![[FT_2025-10-05 22:23:02.702.png | 800x500]]
	- Exam Tip: This second condition is key. It means a diagonal connection is only allowed if there's no 4-connected path between the pixels through their common neighbors.
		- It means always prefer 4-connected even if there is 8-connected.



**Distance measures between pixels**
- **Eucledain Distance**
	- Let p and q be pixels with coordinates (x,y) and (s,t) respectively.
	- Euclidean distance $$D_e(p,q) = [(x-s)^2 + (y-t)^2] $$ 
- **City Block Distance**
	- $$D_4(p,q) = |x-s| + |y-t|$$
			![[FT_2025-10-06 03:06:19.505.png]]

- ***Chessboard Distance*
	- $$D_8(p,q) = max(|x-s| , |y-t|)$$
		![[FT_2025-10-06 03:10:47.254.png]]

- **Dm Distance**
	- It is defined as the shortest m-path between two pixels. This distance depends on the values of the pixels in the path as well as their neighbors.
- ***Important Point***
	- The D4 and D8 distance between p and q are independent to the number of path that might exist between the points because these distance involve only the coordinates of the point.
