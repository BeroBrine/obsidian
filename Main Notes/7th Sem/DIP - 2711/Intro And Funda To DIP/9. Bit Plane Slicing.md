## 9. Bit Plane Slicing
- It is possible to transform a gray level image into a sequence of binary images by taking advantage of its bit patterns storage.


**Concept**
- Suppose a gray level image is stored as an 8-bit Image. Most significant bit has the greatest impact on the image formation. 



- Uses 
	- Steganography -> hiding images within images.
	- Watermarking.
